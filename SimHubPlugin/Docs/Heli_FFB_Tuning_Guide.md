# Helicopter FFB Tuning Guide (MSFS rotor cues)

A practical, fly-and-tune guide for the cyclic, collective and pedal cues
generated by `heli_unboosted_msfs.json` and its embedded sub-graphs. Every
value below is a default — they're starting points, not prescriptions. The
whole point of the design is that you can drag a slider mid-flight, so do
that.

This guide reflects the **Plan 21 physics overhaul** (the "real cues" pass):
torque normalization fix, collective-gated VRS, descent-gated slap, stick-
force-per-G, high-mu buzz, flat-pitch springs on pedals and collective,
ground cues (resonance / touchdown / rumble), turbulence buffet, and a TR-
regime pedal buffet. Several old behaviors were removed — those are flagged
where relevant.

> **Convention.** All parameters appear as graph-editor sliders grouped by
> their `SignalGroup`:
> - **Aircraft.*** — physical/aero properties of the airframe (shared across
>   all axes)
> - **Cyclic.*** — gain on the cyclic stick (pitch *and* roll share these)
> - **Collective.*** — vibration gain on the collective lever
> - **FlightStickCollective.*** — collective base feel (spring/damper/
>   friction/torque-load)
> - **FlightPedals.*** — pedal base feel + cue gains
> - **Pedal.*** — pedal vibration textures

---

## Where the cues live (editor navigation)

The whole rotor-physics layer is computed in **`_embedded/msfs_derivations.json`**,
which is itself organized as a set of small embedded sub-graphs — **one per
cue**, each an Include node you can double-click to open in isolation:

- **VRS**, **Slap**, **RBS**, **ETL Bump**, **Collective Ratio**,
  **Tail Rotor Inflow**, **Mu Buzz**, **Turbulence**, **Tail Rotor Buffet**.

Shared scaffolding (advance ratio `μ`, descent rate, rpm normalization,
G-load, rotation-sign handling) stays at the top level of the derivations
graph, not inside a per-cue Include. To find a cue's math, open the
derivations graph and double-click the matching Include node; to find a
cue's *gains*, look in the per-axis vibration/force includes
(`heli_vibration_cyclic.json`, `heli_pedals_unboosted.json`, etc.).

Ground cues (resonance, touchdown thump, taxi rumble) live in a separate
shared include, **`_embedded/heli_ground_cues.json`**, used by both the
MSFS and X-Plane templates.

## TL;DR — tuning order

1. **Airframe basics** (`Aircraft.RotorDiameterM`, `BladeCount`,
   `RotationSign`, `Rotor.SpeedNom`, `TRBladeCount`, `TRGearRatio`). Get these
   right *before* you fly. There is **no `MaxTorqueNm`** anymore — torque is
   normalized straight from engine torque % (see §1).
2. **Base feel per axis.** Cyclic spring/damper/friction
   (`Cyclic.SpringGain`, `DamperGain`, `Friction`); collective flat-pitch
   spring (`FlightStickCollective.SpringGain`, `SpringCenter`); pedal flat-
   pitch spring (`FlightPedals.SpringGain`, `SpringOffset`). Fly straight-
   and-level — everything should re-center and damp nicely with no rotor
   cues yet (mute the gains; see Isolating cues below).
3. **Cyclic flapback in cruise** (`Cyclic.LoadGain`, `Aircraft.KSpeedPitch`,
   `KSpeedRoll`). The stick should resist forward pressure at 100 kt.
4. **Stick force per G** (`Cyclic.GLoadGain`, optional `Cyclic.GLoadRollGain`).
   Pull G in a turn/flare — the cyclic should stiffen. Bigger day-to-day cue
   than RBS.
5. **ETL bump** (`Aircraft.EtlPeakKts/EtlWidthKts`, `Cyclic.EtlBumpGain`,
   `Cyclic.EtlBumpLoadGain/EtlBumpLoadRollGain`, `Collective.EtlBumpGain`).
6. **VRS** (`Aircraft.VrsBuffetStart/VrsBuffetFull`,
   `Cyclic/Collective.VrsBuffetGain`, `Cyclic.VrsAttenGain`,
   `Cyclic/Collective.VrsVibGain`, `Aircraft.VrsTauUp/VrsTauDown`). VRS is now
   gated by collective — flat-pitch autos produce no VRS.
7. **RBS** (`Aircraft.RbsThreshold/RbsWidth/RbsMuCoupling`,
   `Cyclic.RbsLoadPitchGain/RbsLoadRollGain`, `Cyclic.RbsVib1Gain/RbsVibNGain`,
   `Collective.RbsHeaveGain`).
8. **High-mu buzz** (`Aircraft.MuBuzzOnset/MuBuzzWidth`, `Cyclic.MuBuzzGain`).
   Only matters near Vne.
9. **Slap** (`Aircraft.SlapOnsetKts/KSlap/SlapMax`, `Cyclic.slap_gain`,
   `Collective.slap_gain`). Now descent-gated — only audible in descending
   passes.
10. **Pedal cues** (`FlightPedals.InflowGain`, `FlightPedals.TrBuffetGain`,
    `Pedal.gain_tr_1rev`). Sideslip trim shift + LTE-precursor kicking.
11. **Ground cues** (`Aircraft.GndResGain`, `TouchdownGain`/`TouchdownVsRef`,
    `RumbleGain`/`RumbleRefKts`). Tune on a run-up, a landing, and a taxi.
12. **Turbulence** (`Cyclic.TurbBuffetGain`, `Collective.TurbBuffetGain`,
    optional `FlightPedals.TurbBuffetGain`). Idles silently in calm air.

Most steps take ~5 minutes of flying once you've found a sane baseline.

## Isolating cues for tuning (mute checkboxes)

Each axis output is a *sum* of independent contributions: a base feel
(spring + damper + friction) plus the cue loads and buffets. Every cue has a
**dedicated gain** — silence that gain and the cue vanishes without touching
anything else.

Gain params expose a **mute checkbox** next to the slider. Tick it and the
runtime substitutes the param's `MuteValue` (0 for every gain) instead of
your tuned value — without overwriting the slider. Untick to restore. State
is per-SimHub-session, not saved to the profile, so an inadvertently muted
cue won't ship with your aircraft profile.

Params with a mute checkbox (`MuteValue = 0`) include every cue gain:
`Cyclic.LoadGain`, `Cyclic.GLoadGain`, `Cyclic.GLoadRollGain`,
`Cyclic.VrsBuffetGain`, `Cyclic.VrsAttenGain`, `Collective.VrsBuffetGain`,
`Cyclic.EtlBumpLoadGain`, `Cyclic.EtlBumpLoadRollGain`,
`Cyclic.RbsLoadPitchGain`, `Cyclic.RbsLoadRollGain`, `Cyclic.TurbBuffetGain`,
`Collective.TurbBuffetGain`, `FlightStickCollective.SpringGain`,
`FlightStickCollective.LoadGain`, `FlightPedals.SpringOffset`,
`FlightPedals.InflowGain`, `FlightPedals.TrBuffetGain`,
`FlightPedals.TurbBuffetGain`, all the vibration gains
(`Cyclic.gain_1rev`/`mass_imbalance`/`track_drift`/`base_nrev`/`base_2nrev`/
`slap_gain`/`EtlBumpGain`/`RbsVib1Gain`/`RbsVibNGain`/`VrsVibGain`/
`MuBuzzGain` and the `Collective.*`/`Pedal.*` analogues),
`Aircraft.GndResGain`, `Aircraft.TouchdownGain`, `Aircraft.RumbleGain`,
and `Pedal.gain_tr_1rev`.

The **envelope-shape** params (e.g. `EtlPeakKts`/`EtlWidthKts`,
`RbsThreshold`, `VrsBuffetStart`, `MuBuzzOnset`, `TouchdownVsRef`,
`RumbleRefKts`) have no mute — they shape something already silenced by
muting its gain. The base **spring/damper/friction** and `Cyclic.Vib1Max`
have no mute either — you need them to fly the test manoeuvres at all.

### Suggested workflow

1. **Silent baseline.** Mute every gain. Fly straight-and-level: the per-
   axis springs (cyclic center, collective flat-pitch, pedal flat-pitch) and
   damping should feel right with no rotor cues at all.
2. **Cyclic flapback load.** Un-mute `Cyclic.LoadGain`; confirm forward-
   pressure feel scales with airspeed and disappears in autorotation.
3. **Stick force per G.** Un-mute `Cyclic.GLoadGain`; confirm the cyclic
   stiffens when you pull G.
4. **ETL, VRS, RBS, mu-buzz, slap** — un-mute each family in turn, tuned in
   the relevant manoeuvre (see sections below).
5. **Pedal cues, ground cues, turbulence** — final layers.

SimHub restart re-enables all cues automatically (mute state doesn't
persist), so there's nothing to remember.

### Debugging aids

- The graph editor's **include preview** shows live values for the per-cue
  Include outputs (`vrs`, `etl_bump`, `rbs`, `mu_buzz`, `tr_buffet_gate`,
  `turb`, `tr_inflow`, etc.). Useful for confirming a cue is firing even
  before you can feel it.
- In the derivations graph, the `clamp` between `mul_vrs_core` and
  `lag_asym_vrs` shows the 0..1 VRS value *after* the collective gate and
  *before* the exit lag — cross-check against your `VrsBuffetStart`.

## 1. Airframe basics

These are physical numbers. Get them right once per aircraft and stop
touching them.

| Param | Slider label | What it is | Default | Range | How to find it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.RotorDiameterM` | Rotor Diameter | Main rotor disc diameter | 8.05 m | 5–25 m | Datasheet (tip-to-tip) |
| `Aircraft.BladeCount` | Blade Count | Number of main rotor blades | 5 | 2–8 | Datasheet |
| `Aircraft.RotationSign` | Rotor Direction | +1 = CCW (US/MD500/AS350), -1 = CW (French/EU) | +1 | +1 / -1 | Rotor from above; CCW = +1 |
| `Aircraft.Rotor.SpeedNom` | Nominal Rotor Speed | Rotor RPM at 100% Nr (sets `rpm_norm = rpm / SpeedNom`) | 400 RPM | 100–1000 | Datasheet / observe at 100% Nr |
| `Aircraft.TRBladeCount` | TR Blade Count | Tail rotor blade count | 2 | 2–6 | Datasheet |
| `Aircraft.TRGearRatio` | TR Gear Ratio | Tail rotor : main rotor RPM ratio | 4.62 | 1–10 | Datasheet |

> **No more `MaxTorqueNm`.** The old MSFS template ran torque through an
> Nm round-trip (`%→Nm→÷TorqueNom`), which double-normalized and left the
> collective torque-load 20% weak with two coupled sliders. The MSFS template
> now feeds engine torque **percentage straight into the normalized path** —
> 100% engine torque ⇒ `torque_norm = 1.0`. There is nothing to tune here;
> the `Max Rotor Torque` slider is gone. (The X-Plane template still uses
> real Nm and keeps `Rotor.TorqueNom`.)

> **`rpm_norm` and Ω² scaling.** `rpm_norm = MainRotor.Speed / Rotor.SpeedNom`.
> Aerodynamic and centrifugal amplitudes now scale with **rpm²** (track
> drift, tail-rotor vibration amplitudes, and the pedal spring all use
> `rpm_norm²`, joining the 1/rev mass-imbalance term that already did). At
> nominal rpm the defaults are unchanged; the difference is only audible
> during spool-up/down, where the cues now correctly fade with the square of
> rotor speed instead of linearly.

> **Tip speed is computed**, not configured: `Vtip = RotorDiameterM ×
> MainRotor.Speed × π·1.944/60` knots each frame, so advance ratio `μ =
> TAS/Vtip` climbs correctly when the rotor under-speeds.

### Aircraft-class starting points

| Class | Example | RotorDiameter | BladeCount | RotationSign |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light 2-blade | R22 | 7.7 m | 2 | +1 |
| Light 5-blade | MD 500E | 8.05 m | 5 | +1 |
| Medium twin | H145 | 11.0 m | 4 | +1 |
| Light French | AS350 | 10.7 m | 3 | **-1** |
| Heavy | UH-60 | 16.4 m | 4 | +1 |

> **Airspeed convention.** All speed-sensitive cues use `MSFS.Speed.TAS`
> (true airspeed). At altitude TAS > IAS, so ETL/Slap/RBS/mu-buzz trigger at
> a lower indicated airspeed — which matches real helicopters. **Reverse
> flight is supported** (TAS can go negative): the flapback load sign flips,
> while ETL/Slap gate to forward velocity and stay dormant.

## 2. Per-axis base feel

Before any cue, each axis has a spring/damper/friction base. These have no
mute — you need them to fly.

### 2a. Cyclic

| Param | Slider label | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Cyclic.SpringGain` | Spring Gain | 0.45 | 0–5 | N/mm |
| `Cyclic.DamperGain` | Damper Gain | 0.5 | 0–5 | N·s/mm |
| `Cyclic.Friction` | Friction | 0.1 | 0–2 | N |
| `Cyclic.TrimStep` | Trim Rate | 5.0 | 0.1–50 | mm/s |
| `Cyclic.TrimMin` | Hat Trim Min | -20 | -50–0 | mm |
| `Cyclic.TrimMax` | Hat Trim Max | 20 | 0–50 | mm |

> **Trim is real equipment.** Hat trim (the `TrimStep` mm/s beep-rate
> accumulator) models real unboosted electric trim — e.g. the MD500's trim
> actuators repositioning the spring anchor. The **one deliberate usability
> exception** to the "real cues only" rule is the **force-trim-release (FTR)
> button**: instant spring re-referencing is a boosted-machine feature
> (Bell 206 style). The friction-only end of the spectrum (R22) has neither
> trim nor FTR — don't tune force cues to match an FTR-equipped feel.

### 2b. Collective — flat-pitch spring

What an unboosted collective actually transmits is the main-rotor blades'
feathering moment — a centrifugal twisting moment toward **flat pitch**.
Collective position *is* blade pitch, so the faithful model is a **position
spring**, not a torque load. (Reality check: release the friction in an R22
and the lever falls.) The old torque-driven `LoadForce ∝ torque` model was
demoted to a small thrust-dependent residual.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FlightStickCollective.SpringGain` | Flat-Pitch Spring | Spring stiffness toward the low-pitch stop (× rpm_norm²) | 0.3 | 0–5 | N/mm @ RPM nom |
| `FlightStickCollective.SpringCenter` | Spring Center (low-pitch stop) | Where the spring null sits | -20 | -50–50 | mm |
| `FlightStickCollective.LoadGain` | Torque Load (residual) | Small residual thrust-dependent load (demoted from the old dominant term) | 0.2 | 0–5 | N @ trq_ref |
| `FlightStickCollective.DamperGain` | Damper Gain | — | 0.5 | 0–5 | N·s/mm |
| `FlightStickCollective.Friction` | Friction | — | 0.3 | 0–5 | N |

Emergent (all real): lever force builds during spool-up (rpm² stiffening),
goes light at flat pitch, "falls" with friction off, lightens entering
autorotation, and **lightens when you lower the lever on approach** even with
torque still up — the failure signature the old torque model got wrong.

### 2c. Pedals — flat-pitch spring

There is **no torque-to-pedal force path** (the planned torque-driven pedal
load was dropped — it would keep pushing during pedal turns toward flat
pitch, where real pedals get *lighter*). What unboosted pedals transmit is
the TR blades' centrifugal twisting moment — a spring toward the **flat-pitch
pedal position** with stiffness ∝ Ω². The "pedal force tracks power"
correlation pilots feel is indirect: high power ⇒ you *hold* more TR pitch ⇒
more spring deflection.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FlightPedals.SpringGain` | Spring Gain | Pedal spring stiffness (× rpm_norm²) | 1.0 | 0–5 | N/mm @ RPM nom |
| `FlightPedals.SpringOffset` | Flat-Pitch Spring Offset | Shifts the spring null (× RotationSign) | 3.0 | -20–20 | mm |
| `FlightPedals.DamperGain` | Damper Gain | — | 0.5 | 0–5 | N·s/mm |
| `FlightPedals.Friction` | Friction | — | 0.1 | 0–2 | N |

> **Tuning rule:** set `SpringOffset` so pedal force ≈ 0 in trimmed,
> ball-centered cruise. The fin unloads the TR in cruise, so the physical
> flat-pitch null sits near cruise trim anyway. Emergent, all real:
> sustained power-pedal force in hover, asymmetric pedal weights, light feet
> in cruise, slack pedals in autorotation and during spool-up, and force
> lightening mid pedal-turn.

## 3. Cyclic flapback in cruise (BladeAlph + LoadGain)

Dissymmetry of lift plus 90° rotor precession tilts the disc **aft**
("flapback"): the pilot has to hold **forward** stick force in cruise. The
lateral analogue (sideslip → lateral disc tilt) fires only with actual
sideslip.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.KSpeedPitch` | Blade Alph Gain (Pitch) | Aft-stick force growth with airspeed | 17 | 0–40 |
| `Aircraft.KSpeedRoll` | Blade Alph Gain (Roll) | Lateral force growth with sideslip (β) | 5 | 0–20 |
| `Cyclic.LoadGain` | Flapping Load Gain | Overall BladeAlph → stick force scale (N/deg) | 0.2 | 0–5 |

Accelerate to ~80 kt straight-and-level: you should hold steady forward
pressure scaling with speed. Dead in cruise → raise `Cyclic.LoadGain`. Too
strong → lower `Aircraft.KSpeedPitch`. The load is multiplied by `rpm_norm`,
so it fades to zero in autorotation.

> **VRS attenuation now covers the whole load.** The `(1 − VrsAttenGain ×
> vrs_eff)` softening factor is applied to the **sum** of flapping + ETL +
> RBS + G loads, not just flapping. So a crisp ETL bump no longer punches
> through an otherwise-mushy VRS stick.

## 4. Stick force per G (cyclic maneuvering load)

In an unboosted helicopter, pulling G stiffens the cyclic — blade pitching
moments grow with disc loading. This is a bigger day-to-day cue than RBS: it
loads up flares, turns, and cyclic climbs. The term is `g_load = G − 1`,
**signed**, so pushovers *lighten* the stick.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Cyclic.GLoadGain` | G Load (Pitch) | Aft (pitch) load per G above 1 (× rpm_norm) | 1.0 | 0–5 | N/G |
| `Cyclic.GLoadRollGain` | G Load (Roll, × mu) | Lateral coning/flapping load, scales with `mu × RotationSign` | 0.0 | 0–3 | N/G |

The roll term ships **silent** (default 0). It models the second-order
coning effect: a coned rotor in forward flight develops lateral flapping
(`b1 ∝ a0·μ`, `a0 ∝ G`) that returns as a lateral cyclic force — the G-driven
sibling of the sideslip flapback. It is **zero in hover regardless of G**
(the `μ` factor) and flips with rotor direction. Try ~0.3 on hardware;
expect ~20–30% of the pitch gradient.

## 5. ETL bump

Effective Translational Lift: the rotor transitions to clean air around
15–25 kt. A short vibration + forward nose-tuck + lateral roll.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.EtlPeakKts` | ETL Bump Peak | Centre of the bump (TAS) | 18 | 5–40 | kt |
| `Aircraft.EtlWidthKts` | ETL Bump Width | Gaussian 1/e half-width | 6 | 2–20 | kt |
| `Cyclic.EtlBumpGain` | ETL Bump Vibration (cyclic) | Vibration amplitude at peak | 0.6 | 0–3 | mm |
| `Collective.EtlBumpGain` | ETL Bump Vibration (collective) | Vibration amplitude at peak | 0.4 | 0–3 | mm |
| `Cyclic.EtlBumpLoadGain` | ETL Bump Load (Pitch) | Aft load on cyclic at peak | 0.5 | 0–3 | N |
| `Cyclic.EtlBumpLoadRollGain` | ETL Bump Load (Roll) | Lateral load at peak (× RotationSign) | 0.4 | 0–3 | N |

Accelerate from hover and hold ~20 kt: short vibration centred on 18 kt
(mostly collective), a growing aft force on the cyclic, and on a CCW rotor a
right-lateral force. Tune `EtlPeakKts`/`EtlWidthKts` for placement/sharpness,
then the four gains for intensity. The roll component is sign-flipped
automatically by `RotationSign` — set the gain positive and trust the sign.

## 6. VRS (Vortex Ring State)

VRS happens in **powered** hover-descent: the rotor falls into its own
downwash, thrust collapses, and the cyclic goes mushy with broadband buffet.
Modeled as a buffet force + an attenuation of the cyclic load + a low-
frequency periodic 1/rev shake.

> **Collective-gated now.** The VRS core is multiplied by the collective
> ratio (already computed for RBS) before the lag. A windmilling rotor has no
> vortex ring, so a flat-pitch autorotation produces **no VRS** even in a
> steep, fast descent. Powered descent keeps the cue.

> **Clamped before the lag.** The VRS core (which saturates at 1.25 for
> headroom) is clamped to 0..1 *before* the asymmetric exit lag, so exiting
> deep VRS no longer wastes ~0.45 s decaying invisibly from 1.25 to 1.0 — the
> felt cue starts fading immediately on the intended exit tau.

### 6a. Buffet character

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.VrsBuffetStart` | VRS Buffet Onset | VRS level where buffet starts (0..1) | 0.2 | 0–1 | — |
| `Aircraft.VrsBuffetFull` | VRS Buffet Saturation | VRS level where buffet is fully developed | 0.8 | 0–1 | — |
| `Cyclic.VrsBuffetGain` | VRS Buffet (Cyclic) | Buffet force on cyclic (both axes) | 1.5 | 0–8 | N |
| `Collective.VrsBuffetGain` | VRS Buffet (Collective) | Buffet force on collective | 1.0 | 0–8 | N |

### 6b. VRS 1/rev periodic shake (new)

Complementary to the stochastic buffet — a strong low-frequency airframe
shudder on the 1/rev channel.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Cyclic.VrsVibGain` | VRS 1/rev shake | `vrs × gain × rpm_norm` into the cyclic 1/rev | 0.3 | 0–3 | mm |
| `Collective.VrsVibGain` | VRS 1/rev shake | Same on the collective 1/rev | 0.3 | 0–3 | mm |

### 6c. Cyclic authority loss

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Cyclic.VrsAttenGain` | VRS Load Attenuation | How much VRS suppresses the cyclic load sum | 0.5 | 0–1 |

`(1 − VrsAttenGain × vrs_eff)` multiplies the whole cyclic load sum (§3). At
1.0 with full VRS the cyclic feels dead; at 0 VRS doesn't soften the cyclic.

### 6d. Entry/exit hysteresis

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.VrsTauUp` | VRS Entry Tau | Time constant entering VRS | 0.25 | 0.05–2 | s |
| `Aircraft.VrsTauDown` | VRS Exit Tau | Time constant leaving VRS | 2.0 | 0.5–10 | s |

### Tuning recipe

Hover at 500 ft AGL, **reduce collective until you're descending 1500+ fpm
with zero forward speed** (keep some collective — flat pitch kills the cue
now). You should feel the cyclic soften, broadband buffet on both axes, the
1/rev shake, and a lingering exit. Buffet too quiet → raise the two
`VrsBuffetGain`s; lingers too long → lower `VrsTauDown`; doesn't engage →
check descent rate and `VrsBuffetStart`. (`Aircraft.DescentMaxFpm` /
"VRS Saturation Descent" default 1500 sets where the VRS effect saturates;
`Aircraft.EtlSpeedKts` / "VRS Fade Speed" default 60 kt sets the forward-
speed fade.)

## 7. RBS (Retreating Blade Stall)

High airspeed + high collective + manoeuvre load makes the retreating blade
stall: pitch-up + roll toward the retreating side + a 1/rev (2/rev on R22)
shake. A synthetic envelope:

```
demand = collective_ratio × clamp(G, 0, 3) × (1 + RbsMuCoupling × mu)
RBS    = clamp((demand − RbsThreshold) / RbsWidth, 0, 1)
```

> **Low-G relaxes RBS.** The G term is now `clamp(G, 0, 3)` instead of
> `max(G, 1)`, so a pushover genuinely *reduces* stall demand — matching the
> textbook "unload to exit RBS" recovery. (Capped at 3 to keep turbulence
> spikes bounded.)

### Derivation params

| Param | Slider label | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.RbsMuCoupling` | RBS Mu Coupling | 2.5 | 0–10 |
| `Aircraft.RbsThreshold` | RBS Threshold | 1.6 | 0.5–5 |
| `Aircraft.RbsWidth` | RBS Width | 0.4 | 0.05–3 |

### Consumer gains

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Cyclic.RbsVib1Gain` | RBS 1/rev Vibration | 1/rev shake at full RBS | 0.6 | 0–3 | mm |
| `Cyclic.RbsVibNGain` | RBS N/rev Vibration | N/rev (scales as RBS²) | 0.4 | 0–3 | mm |
| `Collective.RbsHeaveGain` | RBS Heave Vibration | Heave on collective | 0.5 | 0–3 | mm |
| `Cyclic.RbsLoadPitchGain` | RBS Load (Pitch) | Aft load (pitch-up tendency) | 1.2 | 0–5 | N |
| `Cyclic.RbsLoadRollGain` | RBS Load (Roll) | Lateral load toward retreating side (× RotationSign) | 0.8 | 0–5 | N |

> **Collective RBS heave moved to the N/rev channel.** It previously sat on
> the 2N/rev (~67 Hz) harmonic where stall heave read as a high buzz; it now
> rides the N/rev sum like the cyclic RBS shake, where a low-frequency heave
> belongs.

### Tuning recipe

Accelerate to ~110 kt at moderate collective, then pull to ~1.5 G. Expect a
1/rev shake (2/rev on R22), collective heave, shrinking forward-pressure
budget (pitch-up), and on a CCW rotor a left-lateral force. Fires in normal
cruise → raise `RbsThreshold`; never fires → lower it / raise `RbsMuCoupling`.
There is no hysteresis on RBS — it fades within a second on recovery.

## 8. High-mu compressibility buzz

Above `mu ≈ 0.32` the advancing blade picks up a growing N/rev buzz toward
Vne — the only high-speed cue besides the static flapback gradient.
`mu_buzz = clamp((mu − MuBuzzOnset) / MuBuzzWidth, 0, 1)`, fed into the cyclic
N/rev sum.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.MuBuzzOnset` | Mu Buzz Onset | Advance ratio where buzz starts | 0.32 | 0.2–0.5 | — |
| `Aircraft.MuBuzzWidth` | Mu Buzz Width | Ramp width to full buzz | 0.08 | 0.02–0.2 | — |
| `Cyclic.MuBuzzGain` | High-speed (mu) buzz | N/rev amplitude at full buzz | 0.3 | 0–3 | mm |

At `mu = 0.30` there's no buzz; at `mu = 0.40` full `MuBuzzGain` on the N/rev.
If it never appears, your aircraft simply never reaches the onset advance
ratio — lower `MuBuzzOnset` to feel it sooner.

## 9. Slap (BVI) — descent-gated

Blade-Vortex Interaction, the "wop-wop." Real slap peaks in **shallow powered
descents** and flares, not steady cruise. Slap is now multiplied by a descent
gate (`min(descent_fpm / 600, 1)`) before the cap, so it's silent in level
cruise and audible in a descending pass.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.SlapOnsetKts` | Slap Onset Speed | TAS at which slap starts | 20 | 0–80 | kt |
| `Aircraft.KSlap` | Slap Gain | Slap ramp gradient (retuned up) | 0.15 | 0–0.5 | — |
| `Aircraft.SlapMax` | Slap Cap | Cap on slap envelope | 0.1 | 0–1 | — |
| `Cyclic.slap_gain` | N/rev slap (BVI) boost | Slap → cyclic N/rev | 1.0 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Collective.slap_gain` | N/rev slap (BVI) boost | Slap → collective N/rev | 0.5 | 0–2.55 | mm |

`KSlap` was raised from the old 0.03 to **0.15** (max 0.5) so the cue is
actually felt in a descending pass. Vibration-only — no load coupling. Tune
on a ~60 kt / ~500 fpm descent.

## 10. Pedal cues (sideslip + LTE precursor)

Beyond the flat-pitch spring (§2c), pedals carry two small cues plus a
vibration texture.

### 10a. TR inflow sideslip load

Sideslip changes the axial inflow through the TR disc, shifting blade
feathering moments — felt mostly as a **trim-force shift** when the ball is
off-center. `tr_inflow = beta_deg × min((TAS/60)², 1)`.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FlightPedals.InflowGain` | TR Inflow Load | `InflowGain × tr_inflow × RotationSign × rpm_norm²` | 0.05 | -0.5–0.5 | N/deg |

**Signed and small.** The true direction depends on delta-3 / pitch-horn
geometry — flip the sign on hardware if it pushes the wrong way.

### 10b. TR-regime buffet (LTE precursor)

The most distinctive real pedal cue after the spring gradient: irregular
kicking when the TR inflow goes dirty (downwind / critical-side-crosswind
hover, high TR pitch demand). Gated by **low speed × (critical-side sideslip
+ high TR pitch demand)**:

- `lowspeed = clamp(1 − TAS/30, 0, 1)`
- `crit_beta = clamp(β × RotationSign / 15°, 0, 1)` (one-sided — critical
  quadrant only)
- `demand = clamp((tr_pitch_pct − 70) / 30, 0, 1)` (blade-stall proxy)

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FlightPedals.TrBuffetGain` | TR Regime Buffet (LTE) | Buffet amplitude when gated on | 1.0 | 0–10 | N |

Buffet fires **only** when TAS < 30 kt AND (critical-side sideslip OR TR pitch
above 70%). At 50 kt it's zero regardless; sideslip on the safe side
contributes nothing.

### 10c. Pedal vibration textures

| Param | Slider label | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Pedal.gain_1rev` | Main rotor 1/rev coupling | 0.02 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Pedal.gain_nrev` | Main rotor blade-passing coupling | 0.05 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Pedal.gain_tr_blade` | TR blade-passing (primary) | 0.5 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Pedal.gain_tr_2nd` | TR 2nd harmonic | 0.2 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Pedal.gain_tr_1rev` | TR 1/rev (track/balance) | 0.05 | 0–2.55 | mm |

`Pedal.gain_tr_1rev` is the new TR once-per-rev (track/balance) imbalance term
on the previously-unused 5th vibration slot (`HarmRatio5 = tr_gear`,
amplitude × rpm_norm²).

## 11. Ground cues

On the ground these three cues add presence and a strong lift-off / landing
contrast. They live in `heli_ground_cues.json` and use the `MSFS.OnGround`
and `MSFS.GroundSpeed` signals.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Aircraft.GndResGain` | Ground Shake Boost | Multiplies the 1/rev–N/rev sums by `1 + on_ground × GndResGain`; its *disappearance* at lift-off is the cue | 0.6 | 0–3 | — |
| `Aircraft.TouchdownGain` | Touchdown Thump (@ ref VS) | One-shot thump on the on-ground rising edge, stretched into a ~0.3 s decay | 2.0 | 0–10 (template) | N |
| `Aircraft.TouchdownVsRef` | Touchdown VS Reference | Descent rate at contact that produces a full-strength thump (thump scales with sink rate / this) | 300 | 50–1000 | fpm |
| `Aircraft.RumbleGain` | Ground Rumble | Taxi rumble amplitude, scaling with ground speed | 1.0 | 0–100 | N |
| `Aircraft.RumbleRefKts` | Ground Rumble Ref Speed | Ground speed at which rumble reaches full strength | 25 | 5–80 | kt |

> **Ground resonance / lift-off.** With weight on wheels and the rotor
> turning, the airframe transmits a strong 1/rev–N/rev shake that **vanishes
> at lift-off** — the disappearance *is* the lift-off cue. rpm scaling is
> inherent, so a ground run-up builds the shake naturally as Nr spools up.

> **Touchdown thump** is scaled by descent rate at contact (via
> `TouchdownVsRef`): a firm arrival thumps harder than a feathered one.

> **Ground rumble** is taxi feel — it needs `MSFS.GroundSpeed` and scales
> from 0 at a standstill to full at `RumbleRefKts`.

Tune on a run-up (shake builds), a landing (thump + shake drop on lift-off /
arrival), and a taxi (rumble vs ground speed).

## 12. Turbulence buffet

Gusts shake the disc; the pilot feels it in the cyclic before the airframe
visibly moves. There is no wind signal, so it's derived from G via a high-
pass: `turb = |G − lag_asym(G, 0.5, 0.5)|`, added onto the cyclic/collective
buffet (and optionally pedals).

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Cyclic.TurbBuffetGain` | Turbulence Buffet | Cyclic buffet from the G high-pass | 1.0 | 0–10 | N/G |
| `Collective.TurbBuffetGain` | Turbulence Buffet | Collective buffet | 1.0 | 0–10 | N/G |
| `FlightPedals.TurbBuffetGain` | Turbulence Buffet | Optional pedal buffet | 0.0 | 0–10 | N/G |

Ships at 1.0 on cyclic/collective (quiet in calm air, since the G high-pass
idles near 0) and 0.0 on pedals. It also picks up abrupt manoeuvre G
transients — acceptable, since real discs shake in sharp manoeuvres too.

## 13. Cyclic / collective vibration baseline (rotor texture)

Always-on rotor harmonic textures — the cabin "running" vibration.

| Param | Slider label | What it changes | Default | Range | Units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Cyclic.gain_1rev` | 1/rev gain | 1/rev amplitude scaling with BladeAlph (clamped by `Vib1Max`) | 0.5 | 0–2.55 | mm/deg |
| `Cyclic.mass_imbalance` | 1/rev mass imbalance | 1/rev from blade mass imbalance × rpm² | 0.05 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Cyclic.track_drift` | 1/rev track drift | 1/rev from rotor tracking × rpm² | 0.1 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Cyclic.base_nrev` | N/rev base | Always-on N/rev × rpm_norm | 0.3 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Cyclic.base_2nrev` | 2N/rev base | Always-on 2N/rev | 0.1 | 0–2.55 | mm |
| `Cyclic.Vib1Max` | 1/rev cap | Hard cap on the summed 1/rev amplitude (insurance against firmware saturation near Vne) | 2.0 | 0.1–5 | mm |

Collective analogues: `Collective.gain_1rev` (0.05), `Collective.base_nrev`
(0.3), `Collective.base_2nrev` (0.1) plus its own `slap_gain`/`EtlBumpGain`/
`RbsHeaveGain`/`VrsVibGain`.

> **`Cyclic.Vib1Max` (new).** The 1/rev product (`gain_1rev × |blade_alph|`)
> grows unbounded with speed; this clamps the summed 1/rev amplitude before
> it reaches the oscillator, so high-speed flight can't drive firmware-side
> amplitude saturation artifacts. No mute — it's a safety clamp.

## Sanity-check matrix

| Manoeuvre | Expected feel |
|---|---|
| Hover, no descent | Centering/flat-pitch springs, mild N/rev texture, no buffet |
| **Ground run-up** | Growing 1/rev–N/rev shake as Nr spools up; pedals/collective slack → loaded |
| **Lift-off** | Ground shake drops out (the cue) |
| Hover + 1500+ fpm **powered** descent | Cyclic softens, broadband buffet + 1/rev shake |
| **Flat-pitch auto** descent | **No VRS** (collective-gated); pedals + collective go light |
| Accel through 18 kt | Short bump (mostly collective), forward+lateral push on cyclic |
| Pull G in a turn/flare | Cyclic stiffens (G-load); pushover lightens it |
| Cruise 100 kt | Forward stick force, no slap (level), no buffet, no RBS |
| Descending pass ~60 kt | Slap "wop-wop" appears |
| 1.5 G pull at 110 kt | 1/rev shake (2/rev on 2-blade), aft + retreating-side resistance |
| Near Vne (high mu) | N/rev mu-buzz |
| Downwind / critical-side hover | Pedal kicking (TR-regime buffet) |
| Off-center ball in cruise | Small pedal trim-force shift (TR inflow) |
| **Touchdown** | Thump scaled by sink rate |
| Taxi | Rumble scaling with ground speed |
| Autorotation (low RPM) | Loads fade with rpm/rpm²; springs slacken |

## Troubleshooting

**"No force at all"** — confirm the per-axis spring/damper are nonzero and
the ESP32 hardware damping/friction aren't muting everything downstream.

**"Stick fights me everywhere"** — too much damping, friction, or load gain.
Halve everything, add back one term at a time.

**"VRS never engages"** — check `Rotor.VRS` is rising in the include preview.
Remember it's now **collective-gated**: a flat-pitch auto produces none by
design. Also check descent rate vs `DescentMaxFpm`.

**"Collective keeps pulling as I lower it on approach"** — that's the old
torque model; confirm `FlightStickCollective.SpringGain` is nonzero and the
torque `LoadGain` residual is small (default 0.2). The faithful feel is the
position spring lightening as you lower the lever.

**"Pedals push the wrong way in sideslip / off-center in cruise"** — flip the
sign of `FlightPedals.InflowGain`, and re-tune `FlightPedals.SpringOffset` so
force ≈ 0 ball-centered in cruise.

**"Slap is silent"** — it's now descent-gated; you won't feel it in level
cruise. Fly a ~60 kt / 500 fpm descent, and check `KSlap` (default 0.15).

**"RBS / mu-buzz fires constantly"** — raise `RbsThreshold` / `MuBuzzOnset`
for this aircraft's collective/G/speed profile.
